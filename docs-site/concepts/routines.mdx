---
title: "Routines"
description: "Agents that run on a schedule and deliver work while you sleep."
---

A **routine** is an agent with a schedule. Instead of you messaging it, it fires
on its own, on a cron expression or a simple interval, runs its task, and can
alert you with the result.

## Build it in a chat, then schedule it

The reliable way to create a good routine is to **get it working in chat first**,
then schedule it:

1. In **Chat**, iterate on the task until the agent does exactly what you want.
2. Click **Save as routine**, the builder is prefilled with the instruction,
   tools, and data from your chat.
3. Pick a schedule and hit **Create & test run** to confirm it before it goes live.

This beats configuring a routine from scratch, you've already seen it succeed.

## Scheduling

Choose a schedule when you create or edit a routine:

- **Presets** — friendly options like "every weekday at 8am" or "every hour".
- **Cron** — a standard cron expression for full control.
- **Interval** — run every _N_ seconds.

Routines can be **paused** and **resumed** without losing their configuration,
and the console shows the **next run** time.

<Warning>
  Ask an agent in a normal chat to "set up a cron job" and it can't, scheduling
  lives in the platform, not inside a sandboxed run. Use **Save as routine**
  instead; the agent is guided toward that path.
</Warning>

## Alerts

Add **alert rules** to a routine so you're notified about its runs, for example,
only when it fails, or every time it finishes. Alerts appear in the console's
Alerts view.

## Interacting with a routine

A routine is still an agent, so you can open it and **chat with it directly** (a
turn against the same agent, its context, tools, and workspace) to inspect or
adjust what it's doing, without waiting for the next scheduled run.
